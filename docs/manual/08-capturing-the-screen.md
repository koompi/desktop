# Capturing the screen

## Screenshots

Press `Print`.
The screen freezes and you drag a box around what you want, or take the whole thing.

| Keys | Takes |
| --- | --- |
| `Print` | a box you drag, or the whole screen |
| `Super+Shift+S` | the same, without leaving the keyboard's reach |
| `Shift+Print` | the whole screen, no questions |

Every one of these both saves a file and puts the picture on your clipboard, so you can paste it straight into a message.
The files go to `~/Pictures/Screenshots`.

## Pulling things off the screen

Three of these take a box the same way, and give you something other than a picture.

| Keys | Gives you |
| --- | --- |
| `Super+Shift+X` | the text inside the box, on your clipboard |
| `Super+Shift+T` | that text, translated |
| `Super+Shift+A` | an image search for what is in the box |

`Super+Shift+X` is the one worth remembering.
A slide, a scanned page, a photograph of a whiteboard: drag a box around the words and they are text you can edit.

`Super+Shift+C` picks the colour under the pointer and puts `#RRGGBB` on your clipboard.

## Recording

| Keys | Records |
| --- | --- |
| `Super+Shift+R` | a box you drag, no sound |
| `Ctrl+Alt+R` | the whole screen, no sound |
| `Super+Shift+Alt+R` | the whole screen, with sound |

Press the same keys again to stop.
Recordings go to your Videos folder.

There is also a **Screen record** tile in the right-hand panel, which starts and stops the same recording without a keyboard.
